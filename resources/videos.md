# Videos and courses

| Resource | Length | Level | Use with | What it contributes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3Blue1Brown, [*Neural networks*](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks) (series) | about 1.5 h for chapters 1–4; later chapters cover attention and Transformers | introductory | before Module 01; chapters on Transformers with Module 05 | Geometric intuition for gradient descent, backpropagation and attention. |
| Karpathy, [*The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation: building micrograd*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMj-3S1tku0) | 2.5 h | introductory | Module 01 | Autograd from nothing. If Module 01's manual backward pass felt fast, watch this. |
| Karpathy, [*Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCc8FmEb1nY) | 2 h | introductory | Modules 05–06 | The whole model on screen. The single most useful video for Part I. |
| Karpathy, [*Let's build the GPT Tokenizer*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zduSFxRajkE) | 2 h | introductory | Module 03 | BPE implemented and its consequences discussed. |
| Karpathy, [*Let's reproduce GPT-2 (124M)*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l8pRSuU81PU) | 4 h | intermediate | Modules 07–09, 13 | A full training run built up with every efficiency measure explained (precision, compilation, batching, distributed training). nanochat grew out of this line of work. |
| Karpathy, [*Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI) | 3.5 h | introductory | before Part IV, or as an overview at any time | The whole life cycle for a general audience: pretraining, fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, tools, and the limits of each. |
| Stanford [CS224N: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/) | course | intermediate | Parts I–II | A university course with lecture videos, notes and assignments. Stronger than this course on linguistics and on the history of the field. |
| Stanford [CS336: Language Modeling from Scratch](https://cs336.stanford.edu/) | course | intermediate–advanced | after this course, or alongside Parts I–III | Builds tokenizer, model, optimizer and systems components in demanding assignments. The closest university counterpart to this course's approach. |

Watching is not doing. The course's rule applies to videos too: pause before each result
and predict it.
